A letter for you

words I have wanted to say all year

My love,

I keep thinking about how strange it is that the most ordinary day of the year for everyone else is the most important one for me. The day you came into the world is the day my whole life started waiting for the moment I'd find you.

You teach me how to love every day. Not in grand declarations — in small, steady ways. The way you remember things about me I forgot I told you. The way you make tea twice in a row without complaining when I forget mine on the counter. The way you hold my hand harder when I'm worried, even before I've said why.

This year I want to give you the kind of birthday you give to other people. The thoughtful, planned, looked-after kind. I want you to feel the way you make everyone else feel.

Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for laughing at my bad jokes. Thank you for being the person I'm coming home to forever.

Happy birthday, my whole heart. The day belongs to you, and so do I.

— Yours, always
